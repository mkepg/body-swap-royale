What changed from v1, and why

Added a third color family. v1's colors were all "pretty light." The broadcast-glitch family (Neon Buzz, Static) uses HARD cuts — steps, not tweens — a motion signature no smooth-fading color can be confused with, and it's the first cosmetic lane that could only belong to a game-show world. Duskfall brings the world's own sunset into the halo.

Styles now tell the game's story. v1's styles were good geometry but generic (any game could ship Ring/Pulse). v2 adds four homages with real lore: Hexline (the arena), Marquee (the show), Turbine (the sweeper monument), Constellation (the festival night sky) — and Gemini, two souls endlessly trading places: the entire game in one halo, and the same ⇄ motif as the shipped store icon. It replaces Seraph as the legendary style; Seraph was cut — ornate but story-less, and its shard-ring read as noise at 24px next to Gemini's clean two-dot swap.

Trails got verticality and behavior. v1 trails were all horizontal ribbons of different widths. v2 adds the feet plane (Skates), the vertical plane (Cape), rotation (Helix), and state-reactivity (Skybound — airborne-only ignition, reading jump state client-side; zero server involvement, zero fairness surface: it shows information everyone already sees). Meteor demoted to Epic; a legendary should be a mechanic, not a bigger ribbon.
