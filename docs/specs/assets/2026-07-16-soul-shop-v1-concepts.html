Soul Shop — final cosmetic concepts (slice 1)

19 items · 3 categories · every animation below is the real motion design. Click any item to flag it for revision; leave feedback in the terminal.

Colors: "light the free palette can't make"The 8 free colors are pure saturated hues. Premium colors are pales, darks, metallics, and living colors that animate — a visibly different class, never a near-duplicate.
Styles: frames for YOUR colorA style never has its own color — it restructures how your equipped color is worn. Any style × any color composes. All pure billboard UI: zero particles, identical cost to the free dot, mobile-safe at 16 players.
Trails: your color in motionTrails inherit your soul color automatically (identity stays coherent across swaps — the whole trail recolors the instant control changes). Premium = shape and behavior, ≤2 Trail instances per body.
Cut during iteration: Graphite (dark gray halo illegible against the dusk sky), static Gold (collided with free yellow at 24px — reborn as animated Gilded), Spike Crown style (shard arc turned to mush at the 24px other-player size), 3D part-based halos (breaks AlwaysOnTop consistency + adds per-body render cost the billboard system doesn't have).
