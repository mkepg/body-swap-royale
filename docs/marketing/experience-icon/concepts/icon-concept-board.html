Experience Icon · Concept Board

The Icon Must Say
"We Swap Bodies"

The core loop is random control swaps every 30 seconds — you get flung into another player's body. master8 reads as a single spooky possession, not a swap party game. Three directions to fix that, each shown large and at true 64px (the size that actually ships).

Constraint that killed the old "two-body" idea: more subjects = smaller faces at 64px. Every concept below is judged against that.
64px
CONCEPT A

Split Soul

One face · two identities

A single big blocky head split down the middle — warm avatar on one side, cool avatar on the other — joined by a glowing cyan swap seam with a soul at the top. One giant face says "two players share one body," which is exactly the swap, in a single dominant subject.

Pros

  • Huge single face → best 64px read
  • "Two-in-one" = instantly a swap
  • Iconic, ownable silhouette

Cons

  • Most stylized / least literal
  • Split can look "glitchy" if muddy
  • Furthest from master8 we built
64px: strongest Recommended
64px
CONCEPT B

Soul Trade

Two heads · souls crossing

Two blocky characters, and their cyan souls arc and cross in an X with a bright flash where they trade — each player's soul flying into the other's body. The most literal, honest depiction of the mechanic.

Pros

  • Unmistakably "two players swap"
  • Dynamic, energetic motion
  • Shows the multiplayer angle

Cons

  • Two faces = smaller at 64px
  • The X can get busy — needs restraint
  • Twice the render to keep on-model
64px: workable if bold
64px
CONCEPT C

Soul-Jump Hero

Keep master8 · add the origin

Keep our proven master8 hero face, but the soul now clearly arcs in from a second, smaller body it just left in the corner — "your control just jumped into this body." Preserves everything we built; adds the swap story with one extra element.

Pros

  • Reuses the winning master8 render
  • Strong hero face keeps 64px punch
  • Lowest-risk iteration

Cons

  • Swap is implied, not literal
  • Corner body is tiny at 64px
  • Still leans "possession-ish"
64px: safe
My lean: A (Split Soul) for the clearest swap-at-64px, with B (Soul Trade) as the bolder, most literal option. Click a concept above, or just tell me in the terminal. We can also blend — e.g. a Split-Soul face with crossing-soul energy.
